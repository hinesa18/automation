AutoType — Install on a Mac

Requires macOS 13 Ventura or later. Supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
No Xcode, Python, signing certificate, or Terminal commands are needed to use it.

1. Open the downloaded AutoType disk image.
2. Drag AutoType onto the Applications shortcut. If updating, quit AutoType first.
3. Open AutoType from Applications. The app appears as AT in the menu bar.
4. This friend build is not notarized by Apple. If macOS blocks it, open
   System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll to the blocked AutoType notice,
   click Open Anyway, and confirm. Try opening the app first so the notice appears.
   Apple's instructions: https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445
5. Enable AutoType in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility.
   Enable Input Monitoring too if requested. Quit and reopen AutoType if needed.
   Screen capture permission is only needed for the screen-text capture feature.
6. In a blank TextEdit document, type ==autotype== to open setup, or use AT > Start.
   Enter a short sample, choose speed, then Start. Escape stops typing and copies
   the remaining text to your clipboard. Test your intended editor with a short
   sample before using a longer document.

Keep AutoType in Applications. After an update, you may need to remove and re-add
AutoType in Accessibility because this release uses an ad-hoc signature.

To quit: AT > Quit. To uninstall: quit, delete Applications/AutoType.app, and
remove its entries from Privacy & Security. No login item is installed.

This is an initial friend-testing release. Automated checks cover internal
logic; typing, permissions, and OCR on your Mac still require a live check.
